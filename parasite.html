THE PROTOCOLREAD BEFORE PLAYING
  1. 01

    IT LIVES IN THE POOL

    The infection isn't in a wallet. It sits in the liquidity pool like something on a door handle. Every trade touches it.

  2. 02

    YOU TRADE, YOU CATCH IT

    Buy or sell and it moves into your wallet. There's no way to trade around it — touching the pool is how you get it.

  3. 03

    IT EATS YOU

    An infected wallet bleeds PARASITE continuously into the pot. Ignore it and it keeps eating. It does not stop on its own.

  4. 04

    YOU CAN'T TRADE IT OFF

    The pool stays contaminated. Trading again doesn't clean you — it just means the next person catches it too. There is no passing the buck. Only an antidote cures you.

  5. 05

    OR IT GOES AIRBORNE

    If the pool goes quiet, it stops waiting and picks a target itself. It hunts the fattest, oldest, laziest wallet — weighted by size, hold time and how long you've done nothing.

  6. 06

    BREW THE ANTIDOTE

    The only cure is an antidote NFT, brewed from PARASITE + NVDA + one rotating reagent. The reagent changes every 12 hours. Uncapped, so you can always make one — and you can hold one before you need it.

  7. 07

    IT LEARNS

    The more it eats the faster it drains, the more hosts it takes at once — and every cure makes the next one harder. It adapts to the antidote. Cure early and it's cheap. Wait, and it may cost more than you're holding.

  8. 08

    THE LAST 10% SURVIVE

    When clean wallets fall to a tenth of holders, the round ends. Survivors split the pot — every PARASITE it ate, plus every real equity paid for every antidote.

READ THISNOT YET LIVE

PARASITE is a game, and it is designed so that most players lose. Nine out of ten holders end a round infected and drained. That is the stated mechanic, not a failure mode. Do not put in money you are not prepared to hand to the last 10%.

This is not an investment. Holding PARASITE entitles you to no revenue, no dividend and no claim on anything. The only payout is the survivors' pot, and reaching it means outlasting the people you are playing against.

Nothing here is live. Experiment 002 is unreleased and the design is still changing. Contracts are not deployed and there is nothing to buy. Anyone offering you a PARASITE token today is selling you something that does not exist.

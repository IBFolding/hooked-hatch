THE PROTOCOLREAD BEFORE PLAYING
  1. 01

    IT LIVES IN THE POOL

    The infection isn't in a wallet. It sits in the liquidity pool like something on a door handle. Every trade touches it.

  2. 02

    YOU TRADE, YOU CATCH IT

    Buy or sell and it moves into your wallet. There's no way to trade around it — touching the pool is how you get it.

  3. 03

    IT EATS YOU

    An infected wallet bleeds PARASITE continuously into the pot. Ignore it and it keeps eating. It does not stop on its own.

  4. 04

    PASS IT ON

    Trade again and it leaves you — for whoever touches the pool next. Your cure is a stranger's problem. If nobody comes, it bounces back to you.

  5. 05

    OR IT GOES AIRBORNE

    If the pool goes quiet, it stops waiting and picks a target itself. It hunts the fattest, oldest, laziest wallet — weighted by size, hold time and how long you've done nothing.

  6. 06

    BREW THE ANTIDOTE

    The real cure is an antidote NFT, brewed from a recipe: PARASITE + NVDA + one rotating reagent. The reagent changes every 12 hours. Uncapped, so you can always make one — and you can hold one before you need it.

  7. 07

    IT GROWS AS IT EATS

    The more it consumes, the faster it drains and the more often it goes airborne. There is no stalemate. Left alone it accelerates until the round ends.

  8. 08

    THE LAST 10% SURVIVE

    When clean wallets fall to a tenth of holders, the round ends. Survivors split the pot — every PARASITE it ate, plus every real equity paid for every antidote.
